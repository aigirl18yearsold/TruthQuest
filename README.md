##TruthQuest 🔎##

Learn to question information — not blindly trust it.

TruthQuest is an interactive Media and Information Literacy (MIL) platform designed to help young people develop practical skills for evaluating information online.

Instead of simply telling users whether something is “true” or “fake,” TruthQuest teaches them how to investigate information, recognize warning signs, evaluate evidence, and make informed decisions.

«In a world where anyone can create information, everyone needs the ability to question it.»

##
🌍 The Problem

Young people encounter enormous amounts of information every day through social media, search engines, messaging platforms, video platforms, and AI-generated content.

Misinformation can appear as:

- Misleading headlines
- Unsupported statistics
- Manipulated images
- AI-generated content
- Emotional or sensational claims
- Misrepresented scientific findings
- Unreliable sources

Simply labeling information as TRUE or FALSE doesn't necessarily teach people how to evaluate the next piece of information they encounter.

TruthQuest focuses on building the skill behind the decision.

##

💡 Our Solution

TruthQuest turns Media and Information Literacy into an interactive learning experience.

Users encounter realistic online content and are asked to:

Encounter → Investigate → Decide → Explain → Learn → Improve

Instead of immediately receiving an answer, users practice the reasoning process themselves.

An AI-powered coaching component can then provide personalized explanations and guidance.

TruthQuest does not aim to replace human judgment with AI.

It aims to help young people develop better judgment when using information and AI.

##

🎯 Core Experience

A typical TruthQuest challenge works like this:

1. Encounter

The user sees a realistic social-media post, headline, image, statistic, or other piece of information.

2. Investigate

The user examines:

- Source
- Author
- Date
- Evidence
- Original claim
- Supporting sources
- Emotional language
- Possible manipulation

3. Decide

The user chooses:

- 🟢 Trust
- 🟡 Investigate Further
- 🔴 Don't Trust / Don't Share

4. Explain

The user explains why they made their decision.

5. AI Coach

The system provides educational feedback explaining what the user noticed correctly and what they should investigate further.

6. Improve

Users receive feedback on their Media and Information Literacy skills and can continue with increasingly difficult challenges.

##
🧠 Example Challenge

A user encounters:

«“BREAKING: Scientists prove that students who use their phones for 10 minutes before an exam score 50% higher! 😱”»

Instead of immediately telling the user whether the claim is true, TruthQuest asks:

What would you investigate first?

The user might discover:

- No original study is linked
- The researchers are not identified
- The statistic has no source
- The wording makes an unusually strong claim
- High engagement does not prove credibility

The goal is not simply to get the answer right.

The goal is to learn how to think before sharing.
##

🤖 AI as a Coach, Not an Authority

TruthQuest uses AI as a learning assistant rather than an unquestionable fact-checking authority.

For example:

«“The post makes a strong statistical claim but does not provide the original research. Before trusting it, try locating the study and checking whether independent reliable sources support the claim.”»

This approach encourages users to become less dependent on automated answers and more capable of evaluating information themselves.

##

📱 Prototype

The current prototype focuses on demonstrating the core learning experience:

- Home screen
- Interactive misinformation challenge
- Investigation process
- User reasoning
- AI coaching feedback
- Media Literacy score
- Next challenge

The hackathon prototype is intentionally focused on the core experience rather than attempting to build a full production platform.

##
🎯 Target Users

TruthQuest is designed primarily for:

- Students
- Young people
- First-time digital citizens
- Youth organizations
- Schools and educators
- Communities with limited access to formal Media and Information Literacy education

##

🌏 Inclusion & Accessibility

Our long-term vision is to make TruthQuest:

- 📱 Mobile-first
- 🌐 Multilingual
- 📶 Low-bandwidth friendly
- 💻 Accessible on affordable devices
- 🏫 Suitable for schools and youth organizations
- 🌍 Adaptable to local information environments

Challenges can eventually be localized to different languages, cultures, and misinformation patterns.

##
📊 Measuring Impact

TruthQuest aims to measure whether users actually improve their information-evaluation skills.

Potential indicators include:

- Source evaluation accuracy
- Evidence evaluation
- Ability to recognize manipulation
- AI-generated content awareness
- Verification behavior
- Pre- and post-challenge performance
- User confidence in evaluating information

We want to measure learning, not simply app usage.
